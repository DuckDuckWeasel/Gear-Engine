02 · Color

Racing energy, readable surfaces

Dark neutrals carry content. Yellow identifies the normal primary action; blue is secondary or informational; red carries brand motion and destructive/error meaning; green confirms success.

Contrast rule: use Asphalt text on Yellow, Blue, Green, and Red fills. Race White on Racing Red is only 3.67:1 and is not suitable for normal-size text. Color must never be the only status cue.

03 · Typography

Fast headings, calm instructions

Oswald supplies compact racing cadence for short display copy. Inter keeps instructions, controls, metadata, and long-form reading clear.

Oswald Variable · Display

Build the drive

Use uppercase only for short headings, labels, and race metrics. Do not set paragraphs or error explanations in condensed type.

Inter Variable · Body and UI

Configure the motor and gears, run the track, inspect the result, and make one informed change before the next race.

Start body copy at 16 px. Use sentence case and direct verbs for controls and instructions.

04 · Interface

One next action per state

Decision load should follow the player's current intent. Keep the next useful action perceptually first, then place supporting actions near the content they affect.

  1. BuildConfigure a valid motor-and-gear system.
  2. RaceWatch the system perform on the current track.
  3. ImproveRead the result and change the next run.

Contextual hierarchy

Controls communicate priority

Race now View storage Delete gear

Yellow is the normal primary action. Blue is secondary/informational. Red requires a destructive or error label—never color alone.

State completeness

Every flow needs recovery

  • Loading states name what is happening and retain orientation.
  • Disabled actions explain what enables them when their presence teaches the sequence.
  • Errors persist until the person can retry or return to a safe state.
  • Navigation and resize preserve meaningful context.

05 · Content

Show the cause and effect

The voice is direct, energetic, and factual: name the action, explain what changes, and show evidence. Concept work must remain visibly separate from the current build.

Do

Make evidence legible

  • Use build, configure, race, observe, and improve.
  • Label media as current build, historical, concept, or promotional composite.
  • Keep enough interface context to prove the feature.
  • Use native video controls and factual poster frames.

Do not

Do not turn direction into a claim

  • Do not present concept maps, rankings, or rewards as shipped gameplay.
  • Do not alter gameplay outcomes, currencies, or telemetry in factual evidence.
  • Do not claim launch, traction, certification, or platform support without proof.
  • Do not autoplay media with sound.

06 · Accessibility

A baseline, not a claim

The portfolio and press-kit experience targets WCAG 2.2 AA. Static inspection cannot establish conformance; keyboard, zoom, reflow, contrast, and assistive-technology paths still require human validation.

Perceivable

Text and evidence

Normal text targets 4.5:1. Meaningful images have descriptive alternatives. Status never depends on color alone.

Operable

Keyboard and touch

Controls remain keyboard accessible, show a 3 px yellow focus outline, and use 44 × 44 px targets for touch-oriented surfaces.

Adaptable

Reflow and motion

Content becomes one column on compact widths without page-level horizontal scrolling, and reduced-motion preferences remove nonessential motion.

07 · Assets and handoff

Use what is approved

The current package is suitable for digital portfolio and press applications. Manufacturing, trademark submission, and long-term production need deterministic masters and documented rights.

Current production boundaries
  • The racing wordmark is an approved raster application, not a verified vector master.
  • The square illustration is JPEG-encoded despite its `.png` filename.
  • The Leonardo Lycan lockup is a separate portfolio identity.
  • Exploratory route marks and concept assets are not default identity assets.
Release handoff checklist
  • Rebuild and approve the racing wordmark as a deterministic vector master.
  • Produce approved monochrome, reversed, reduced, and app-icon variants.
  • Record provenance, ownership, approval date, and modification rights.
  • Verify font distribution rights for each target.
  • Test compact, medium, expanded, keyboard, touch, zoom/reflow, and reduced-motion behavior.
